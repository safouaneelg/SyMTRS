## Read this before reading any number

- **Budget**: 2 epochs (shadow removal: 500 iterations) per model. Nothing is converged.
- **Data**: preview dataset, 144 transforms (96 train / 24 val / 24 test), dataset fingerprint `02b1b436c745c3fc` ([DATASET.md](DATASET.md)).
- **Geographic splits with distribution shift**: the test split is deeper (median depth ~208 m vs ~92 m in train) and class shares differ strongly between splits (val is 63 % road). A generalisation gap here is partly a dataset property, not only a model one. The depth shift has a capture cause: the camera altitude is set above the first surface hit under the location, and both test locations sit on skyscraper roofs, so their street is 152-275 m away against 70-182 m in train/val (DATASET.md).
- **FOV**: the capture camera's horizontal FOV is 60 deg, confirmed by the dataset owner on 2026-10-17 (a car-size estimate over 838 cars, 54-63 deg, agrees); it sets the focal length that DA3-metric (and Depth Pro, `f_px`) use. Zero-shot DA3-metric stays far too small on nadir views at 70-270 m (domain gap, not a unit bug).
- **Shadow-removal inits are heterogeneous**: ShadowFormer starts from ISTD+ (shadow-removal weights), Restormer and Uformer from denoising weights (no shadow weights released), ShadowMaskFormer from scratch (no weights released). The rows are not a fair comparison.
- **Shadow translation (CycleGAN / pix2pix)** trains and predicts at 256 px and is scored at 2048. **KID is the primary metric over FID** (N=24 samples against 2048-d features: the FID covariance is rank-deficient).
- `n/a` = metric absent from that summary (older code) or not computable; never 0.

## shadow_removal (4 runs)

`rgb_day` -> `rgb_noshadow` at 2048. `init` = what the run was initialised from; the models do NOT start from comparable weights (ShadowFormer: ISTD+ shadow removal; Restormer/Uformer: denoising; ShadowMaskFormer: none released, from scratch), so rows must not be ranked. SSIM_y is luma-only SSIM (not comparable with published per-channel SSIM). `MAE LAB` is the ISTD literature's "RMSE": the absolute error summed over L, a and b, so it can exceed `RMSE LAB`, which is a true per-channel root mean square. Shadow mask is derived from the pair (and handed to ShadowFormer / ShadowMaskFormer at test time too, as in ISTD; Restormer/Uformer get no mask). The `identity` row scores the shadowed input itself: a model removes shadows only where it beats that row.
